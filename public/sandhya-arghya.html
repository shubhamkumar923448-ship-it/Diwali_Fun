🌇 Day 3 of the Mahaparv • कार्तिक शुक्ल षष्ठी (संध्या अर्घ्य)

In Reverence of the Setting Sun

Sandhya Arghya

As the day draws to a close, devotees gather at the water's edge to offer Arghya to Surya Dev. With folded hands, sacred offerings and the timeless songs of Chhath, this evening becomes an expression of gratitude for the life, light and energy that sustain us all.

1. घाट पर आगमन एवं तैयारी (Arrival at the Ghat)
2. सूप एवं प्रसाद की सजावट (Arrange the Offerings)
3. अस्ताचलगामी सूर्य को अर्घ्य (Offer Evening Arghya)
🌅 Horizon: Golden Dusk
१. डाला छठ एवं घाट वेदी सजावट

Step 1: Procession to the Crimson Ghat

Carrying bamboo Daura baskets on their heads while singing timeless Chhath Geet, families walk barefoot to the riverbanks. A sacred canopy of five freshly harvested sugarcanes is erected, and hundreds of earthen diyas illuminate the ghat waters.

The Sacred Specifications of Sandhya Arghya

Each offering placed in the bamboo Soop reflects devotion, simplicity and gratitude for the gifts of nature.

🧺

Bamboo Soop & Daura

बाँस का सूप एवं दौरा

Traditionally woven from bamboo, the Soop and Daura hold and carry the offerings of Chhath. The Soop is carefully arranged with fruits, Thekua and other sacred items before being taken to the Ghat.

🍪

Thekua & Rice Laddus

ठेकुआ एवं चावल के लड्डू

Thekua is a traditional Chhath Prasad prepared mainly with wheat flour, jaggery and ghee. Rice flour laddus, also known as Kasar in many households, are among the other offerings prepared with devotion.

🥥

Nature's Seasonal Offerings

नारियल, केला एवं ऋतु फल

Whole coconut, bananas, sugarcane, large lemon, turmeric, ginger and seasonal fruits are among the traditional offerings. Their selection may vary with local customs and availability.

🌇

Worship of the Setting Sun

अस्ताचलगामी सूर्य की आराधना

Chhath holds a special place in Surya worship because devotees offer Arghya to both the setting and rising Sun. Sandhya Arghya expresses gratitude to Surya Dev for the light and energy that sustain all life.
