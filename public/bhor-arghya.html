A Prayer of Gratitude at the First Light

Bhor Arghya

Before the first light touches the horizon, devotees gather at the water's edge to offer Usha Arghya to the rising Surya Dev. With folded hands, they pray to Chhathi Maiya, give thanks for the gift of life, and bring the sacred Nirjala Vrat to its completion through Parana.

1. उषाकाल की प्रतीक्षा (Waiting for Dawn)
2. उदीयमान सूर्य को अर्घ्य (Usha Arghya)
3. व्रत पारण एवं प्रसाद (Parana & Prasad)
🌌 4:30 AM: Brahma-Muhurta Mist
१. ब्रह्म मुहूर्त एवं प्रथम किरण की प्रतीक्षा

Step 1: The Pre-Dawn Vigil in Sacred Waters

Long before sunrise, under a canopy of fading stars and cool river mist, Vrati devotees enter the holy water holding bamboo Soops. Facing East in deep meditation, the entire ghat awaits the arrival of Usha—the Vedic Goddess of Dawn.

The Sacred Specifications of Bhor Arghya

A morning of prayer, gratitude and the sharing of Chhath Mahaprasad.

🌄

Usha Arghya

उदीयमान सूर्य को अर्घ्य

Before sunrise, Vratis stand in the water facing east and offer Arghya to the rising Surya Dev. This sacred prayer expresses gratitude to the source of light, energy and life, while devotees also seek the blessings of Chhathi Maiya.

🙏

Parana: Completion of the Vrat

महाव्रत का पारण

After Usha Arghya, the Nirjala Vrat that began after Kharna is brought to its conclusion. The Vrati breaks the fast according to family tradition, often beginning with water or milk and a small portion of Prasad.

🌺

The Tradition of Sindoor

लंबा सिंदूर लगाने की परंपरा

In many families, married women wear a long line of orange or vermilion sindoor extending from the nose towards the forehead or hairline. It is a cherished regional expression of devotion and auspiciousness.

🍪

Sharing Mahaprasad

महाप्रसाद का वितरण

After the worship, Chhath Prasad such as Thekua, fruits and other sacred offerings are shared with family members, relatives, neighbours and fellow devotees. The joy of receiving and sharing Prasad brings the community together.
