The First Day of Sacred Observance

Nahay Khay

The Chhath Mahaparv begins with Nahay Khay, a day devoted to cleanliness, purity and preparation. Devotees take a sacred bath, prepare a simple Satvik meal and begin the observance with faith and discipline, in reverence to Surya Dev and Chhathi Maiya.

1. पवित्र स्नान (Holy Dip)
2. घर एवं रसोई की शुद्धि (Purifying the Home)
3.सात्विक भोजन/(कद्दू-भात प्रसाद) (Kaddu-Bhat)/(Satvik Meal)
१. गंगा स्नान एवं संकल्प

Step 1: The Sacred River Bath

On Kartik Shukla Chaturthi, devotees begin the Chhath observance with a sacred bath, traditionally in a river or other holy water body. They wear clean clothes and prepare themselves for the days of devotion ahead. Where a river bath is not possible, household traditions may include bathing at home with Gangajal.

The Traditions of Nahay Khay

The first day of Chhath reflects simplicity, cleanliness and devotion through age-old household traditions.

🥣

Traditional Nahay Khay Meal

कद्दू, अरवा चावल और चना दाल

The traditional meal commonly includes kaddu ki sabzi, Arwa rice (unparboiled rice) and chana dal. Prepared with care and simplicity, this Satvik meal marks the beginning of the Chhath observance.

🔥

A Pure Cooking Space

मिट्टी का चूल्हा और आम की लकड़ी

Special attention is given to the cleanliness of the kitchen, cooking vessels and preparation area. Many households traditionally prepare the food on a separate clay stove (Mitti ka Chulha), keeping the ritual meal distinct from everyday cooking.

🧂

Simple & Satvik Ingredients

सात्विक सामग्री एवं शुद्धता

Fresh ingredients, clean utensils and careful preparation are central to the Nahay Khay meal. Many families avoid onion and garlic and follow their own traditions regarding salt, ghee and other ingredients.

🙏

Ekabhukta: One Sacred Meal

सूर्य देव के भोग के पश्चात ग्रहण

In many traditions, the Vrati observes Ekabhukta and partakes of the Nahay Khay meal only once during the day. The meal is prepared and consumed with devotion, marking the beginning of the discipline of Chhath.
