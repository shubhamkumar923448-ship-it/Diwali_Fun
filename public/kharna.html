The Sacred Twilight of Silence

Kharna

A day of fasting from food and water, followed by the sacred evening offering of Gur ki Kheer — Rasiya — and wheat roti. As the Vrati completes the Kharna worship and partakes of the prasad, the approximately 36-hour Nirjala Vrat begins, continuing through Sandhya Arghya and ending after Usha Arghya.

1. दिनभर का निर्जला उपवास (Day-long Fast)
2. गुड़ की खीर एवं रोटी (Preparing Kharna Prasad)
3. संध्या पूजा एवं प्रसाद (Evening Worship & Prasad)
१. दिनभर का निर्जला उपवास

Step 1: A Day of Devotion and Restraint

On Kartik Shukla Panchami, the second day of Chhath, the Vrati observes a day-long fast without food or water. The day is devoted to prayer, discipline and preparations for the evening Kharna worship. As evening approaches, the Vrati prepares for the sacred ritual according to the family's traditions.

The Sacred Specifications of Kharna

An evening of prayer, gratitude and the sacred prasad before the long Nirjala Vrat begins.

🍯

Rasiya — Gur Ki Kheer

गुड़, अरवा चावल एवं दूध

Rasiya is a traditional Kharna prasad prepared by cooking Arwa rice with jaggery and milk. Its preparation is carried out with special care and cleanliness as part of the evening observance.

🫓

Wheat Roti & Prasad

गेहूँ की रोटी एवं घी

Wheat rotis, often prepared with ghee, accompany the jaggery kheer. Bananas and other fruits may also be included in the Kharna offering according to family traditions.

🔥

Traditional Cooking

मिट्टी का चूल्हा एवं आम की लकड़ी

In many households, Kharna prasad is prepared on a clean clay stove using mango wood. A separate, carefully cleaned cooking space and utensils reflect the importance of purity during the observance.

🙏

The Beginning of Nirjala Vrat

खरना प्रसाद के बाद ३६ घंटे का व्रत

After the evening worship and partaking of Kharna prasad, the Vrati begins the approximately 36-hour Nirjala Vrat. No food or water is taken until the fast is concluded after the Usha Arghya on the fourth day.
